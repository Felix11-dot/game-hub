24 GAME

Use four cards to make 24!

Who got 24? Tap to score

How to play

  • Use all four cards exactly once with + − × ÷ and parentheses to make exactly 24.
  • Cards run from Ace to 10 (no face cards). An Ace counts as 1, or 11 if you prefer.
  • Example: 6, 2, 5, 2 → (6 + 2) × (5 − 2) = 24
  • Everyone works it out. The first player to get 24 taps their button to score a point, and the next hand is dealt.
  • Easy: every hand has two or more different solutions. Hard: every hand has exactly one.
